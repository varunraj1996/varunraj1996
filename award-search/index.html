        Transfer partners

        Which card points move to which airline programs, and at what rate. The last column is the most miles you could have in each program using the balances in My points.

        A ratio of 1:0.8 means 1,000 card points become 800 airline miles. Banks move points in blocks of 1,000. Times are typical, not guaranteed. Partner lists change often, so check your card's site before you transfer.

        About this demo

        Waypoint shows how an award search engine works from end to end. Flight schedules, award seats, cash fares and points prices are simulated. They follow the way each program prices awards (fixed charts, distance bands, demand pricing, surcharges) and stay the same for a given route and date, but they are not live airline data.

        Transfer partners and ratios follow what the card programs have published. They change often, so confirm them on your card's site first. Point transfers can't be reversed.

        Not affiliated with any airline, bank or award search service. Your balances stay in this browser.
